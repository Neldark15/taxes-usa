# Other States Overview

Once you master Federal + Virginia, expanding to other states is about learning **differences**, not a new system. Federal is the base; each state adds its layer.

## The 9 states with NO income tax

No state income tax in:

- **Alaska (AK)**
- **Florida (FL)** — high Salvadoran diaspora
- **Nevada (NV)**
- **New Hampshire (NH)** — only taxed interest/dividends (eliminated in 2024)
- **South Dakota (SD)**
- **Tennessee (TN)**
- **Texas (TX)** — large Salvadoran diaspora
- **Washington (WA)** — has excise tax on capital gains
- **Wyoming (WY)**

<Callout type="tip" title="Operational advantage">
If your client lives in FL, TX or any of these, **you don't prepare a state return** — only federal. You charge less but the work is faster.
</Callout>

## Preparer nexus — can I prepare taxes from any state?

**Federal**: any preparer with PTIN can prepare returns for clients in any state.

**States with mandatory preparer registration**:

- **California (CTEC)** — California Tax Education Council. Requires 60h initial course + 20h/year, $5k bond, annual registration. **Mandatory** if you prepare CA resident returns for compensation.
- **New York** — NYTPRIN registration + 4h CE.
- **Maryland** — register with Board of Individual Tax Preparers.
- **Oregon** — LTC/LTP licensing (stricter).
- **Connecticut** — Tax Preparer Permit.
- **Nevada** — mandatory for paid preparers.
- **Illinois** — voluntary.

<Callout type="warning" title="California CTEC">
If you plan to grow the business to Salvadoran clients in Los Angeles, Bay Area or Central Valley, **CTEC is mandatory**. Penalty for preparing without CTEC can be $5,000+ per return.
</Callout>

## State reciprocity — general map

States with reciprocity agreements (your resident works there but files at home):

- **VA ↔ DC/MD/KY/PA/WV** (already covered in ch. 02)
- **MD ↔ DC/PA/VA/WV**
- **NJ ↔ PA**
- **IL ↔ IA/KY/MI/WI**
- **IN ↔ KY/MI/OH/PA/WI**
- **OH ↔ IN/KY/MI/PA/WV**

States without reciprocity force filing in both with **credit for taxes paid to other state** — more work and higher fee.

## Multi-state typical — clients with 2 states

Common diaspora scenarios:

1. **Move during the year** → part-year in both states (e.g., Form 760PY in VA + equivalent in the other)
2. **Cross-state remote work** → state of residence is where they live, but some states apply convenience rule (NY, DE, NE, PA) that force filing in both
3. **Rental property in another state** → nonresident return in property state

## Fee implications

Each additional state return typically adds $50-100 to your pricing. For complex multi-state clients (3+ states), charge more for complexity and time.
