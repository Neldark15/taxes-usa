# Annual Tax Calendar

All the key dates you must know. Mark these in your calendar and set alerts.

<Callout type="info" title="Weekend dates">
If any deadline falls on Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday, it moves to the next business day. Always verify the current year's calendar at irs.gov.
</Callout>

<KeyDate date="January 27" label="IRS opens e-file (approximate)" />
<KeyDate date="January 31" label="W-2 and 1099 to recipients/employees" />

## February

<KeyDate date="February 15" label="W-4 form for exempt employees" />
<KeyDate date="February 28" label="Paper filing deadline for 1099 to IRS" />

**Season heating up**. Most clients come in between Feb 1 and Apr 10.

## March

<KeyDate date="March 1" label="Farmers and fishermen tax due (if they didn't pay estimated)" />
<KeyDate date="March 15" label="S-Corporations (Form 1120-S) and Partnerships (Form 1065)" />
<KeyDate date="March 15" label="S-Corps/Partnerships extensions (Form 7004)" />

**IMPORTANT**: If a client has S-Corp or partnership, the deadline is **1 month before** individual. Don't mix them up.

## April

<KeyDate date="April 15" label="⭐ Form 1040 (personal federal), C-Corp 1120" />
<KeyDate date="April 15" label="Q1 estimated taxes" />
<KeyDate date="April 15" label="Extension request (4868) — personal" />
<KeyDate date="April 15" label="IRA/HSA contributions for prior year" />
<KeyDate date="April 15" label="FBAR (FinCEN 114) — auto-extends to Oct 15" />

<Callout type="danger" title="The most important day of the year">
April 15 is YOUR critical day. Plan to have ALL returns ready before 5pm EST that day. Any stragglers must file 4868 extension.
</Callout>

## May

<KeyDate date="May 1" label="⭐ Virginia Form 760 deadline" />
<KeyDate date="May 15" label="Tax-exempt organizations (Form 990)" />

**May is your post-April breath** but don't relax: VA is due on the 1st.

## June

<KeyDate date="June 15" label="Q2 estimated taxes" />
<KeyDate date="June 15" label="1040-NR without wages (nonresidents)" />
<KeyDate date="June 15" label="US citizens/residents abroad automatic extension" />

## July - August

**Slow season**. Use these months for:

- Review returns pending extension
- CE for AFSP/EA
- Marketing for next season
- File and backup cleanup
- Engagement letter updates

## September

<KeyDate date="September 15" label="Q3 estimated taxes" />
<KeyDate date="September 15" label="S-Corps and Partnerships — extended deadline" />

## October

<KeyDate date="October 15" label="⭐ Form 1040 — final extended deadline" />
<KeyDate date="October 15" label="FBAR — final extended deadline" />
<KeyDate date="October 15" label="C-Corps (1120) extended" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Last real deadline of the year">
October 15 is the absolute final date for most extended returns. After this date, the client is in "late filing" with penalties accumulating.
</Callout>

## November

<KeyDate date="November 1" label="Virginia extended 760 deadline" />

**Quiet month**. Planning for next year.

## December

<KeyDate date="December 31" label="Last day for tax-year decisions" />

- Send "year-end planning" emails to clients

- Wrap up CE credits for the year
