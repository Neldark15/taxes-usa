# Client Workflow + Pricing

The complete service process, from acquisition to payment. This is your "production line".

## Step 1 — Marketing and acquisition

The Salvadoran diaspora is a **referral and trust** market. You don't compete on SEO with H&R Block — you compete to be "Don Carlos's accountant who worked for half of Arlington".

### Effective channels

- **WhatsApp groups** of Salvadorans (Northern VA, Langley Park, Brentwood NY, etc.)
- **Facebook groups**: "Salvadoreños en Virginia", "Salvadoreños en DMV"
- **Referrals**: each happy client brings 2-3 more → offer referral discount ($20 off)
- **Churches and community associations**
- **Remittance companies**: many Salvadorans visit regularly → partnership opportunity
- **Hispanic radio/TV** in Washington DC area

### Don't waste on

- General Google Ads (expensive, noisy)
- Yelp (off-target)
- Printing flyers (low conversion)

## Step 2 — Initial consultation (free, 15 min)

Via video (Zoom/Google Meet) or WhatsApp call. Goals:

- Determine case complexity
- Identify red flags (problematic client, overly complex cases)
- Sell your service
- Give fee estimate

### Basic script

1. Immigration status / document type? (SSN vs ITIN)
2. W-2, 1099, or mixed?
3. State(s) of work and residence?
4. Kids / dependents? Where do they live?
5. Self-employed or own business?
6. Properties? Rentals?
7. Bank accounts in El Salvador? (FBAR alert)
8. Did you file last year? Where's the copy?

## Step 3 — Engagement letter

Signed BEFORE receiving documents. Defines:

- Exact services (1040, state, add-ons)
- Fees and payment method
- Your duties (confidentiality, diligence)
- Client duties (truthful documents, complete information)
- What you DON'T do (audits, prior-year amendments without additional fee)
- Liability limitation
- Communication policy

## Step 4 — Intake form + document collection

You send checklist:

- ID photo (passport / license / ITIN letter)
- W-2s from all jobs
- All 1099s
- 1098-T if college attendance
- Prior year's return
- If 1099/self-employed: income and expense summary
- Dependents list with birth date and SSN/ITIN

**Secure portal mandatory** (TaxDome or similar). NEVER unencrypted email.

## Step 5 — Return preparation

- Enter data into software (Drake/TaxSlayer)
- Review consistency with prior year (catches errors)
- Optimize: standard vs itemized, correct dependents, applicable credits
- **Form 8867** if applicable (EITC/CTC/AOTC/HOH) — MANDATORY

Typical time:

- Simple W-2 1040: 30-45 min
- 1040 + Schedule C: 60-90 min
- Multi-state + ITIN + dependents: 2-4 hours

## Step 6 — Review with client

15-30 min video call:

- Show return summary
- Explain refund or balance due
- Discuss strategies for next year
- Answer questions

## Step 7 — 8879 signature

- Send 8879 via e-signature (DocuSign or TaxDome)
- Client signs within 24-48 hours
- IMPORTANT: **cannot e-file until 8879 is signed**

## Step 8 — E-file + IRS confirmation

- Transmit the return
- Receive IRS acknowledgement (usually under 24h, sometimes instant)
- If rejection: investigate (SSN mismatch, duplicate dependent, etc.) and re-transmit
- Confirm to client: "Your return was accepted by the IRS. Refund expected in 21 days."

## Step 9 — Delivery to client

- Complete PDF of 1040 + schedules + states
- Signed 8879 PDF
- Executive summary (1 page) with key points
- Next steps calendar (estimated taxes, state deadline if applicable)

## Step 10 — Payment

**IMPORTANT**: collect **before or at e-file time**, not after the refund.

### Recommended methods

- **Zelle** (free, US banks only — client pays, you receive if you have access)
- **Wise Business** (USD → USD, low fees)
- **Stripe** (2.9%, professional, allows recurring)
- **Venmo Business** (1.9%)
- **Direct bank transfer**

### Don't use

- **Bank products** (refund advance loans) without formal provider contract — highly regulated
- Checks (too slow, bounce risk)
- Cash (unless Form 8300 if > $10k)

## Step 11 — Follow-up and maintenance

- **IRS notices**: client calls → you help respond
- **Amendments** if errors exist (charge additional fee)
- **Estimated taxes** quarterly reminder (Q1-Q4)
- **Next year reminder**: contact in December for planning
- **Referral ask**: after a happy client, ask for referrals

## Pricing Guide — How to charge for your services

This is **the most important section** for your profitability.

### Circular 230 §10.27 — what's prohibited

<Callout type="danger" title="Contingent fees PROHIBITED">
**You cannot charge a percentage of the client's refund** on original federal returns. This violates Circular 230 §10.27 and can result in IRS suspension/disbarment.

**Limited exceptions**: amended returns under examination, claims for refund, and certain appeals.
</Callout>

Also prohibited:

- **Unconscionable fees**
- **Acceptance of referral fees** from banks for bank products without disclosure
- **Undisclosed fee splitting**

### Allowed pricing structures

1. **Flat fee per form/schedule** ⭐ MOST COMMON
   - Base 1040 + each schedule + each state = total
   - Predictable for client
   - Example: $175 base + $50 Schedule C + $75 state = $300
2. **Hourly**
   - Typical in complex cases (audits, complex business)
   - Range: $100-400/h based on credential
3. **Value-based / bundle**
   - Annual package including taxes + bookkeeping + advisory
   - More common with business clients ($200-500/month)
4. **Hybrid**
   - Flat fee with "+X for additional complexity"

### Reference: NATP Fee Study (US averages)

The **NATP** (National Association of Tax Professionals) publishes an annual fee survey. Typical averages:

| Service | US Average |
|---|---|
| 1040 no itemized + 1 state | $220 |
| 1040 + Schedule A (itemized) | $323 |
| + Schedule C (self-employment) | +$192 |
| + Schedule E (rental) | +$145 |
| + Schedule D (capital gains) | +$118 |
| + Schedule SE | +$42 |
| + Additional state return | +$50-100 |
| 1040-NR | $300-500 |
| W-7 ITIN (with return) | +$100-250 |
| FBAR FinCEN 114 | $100-200 |
| Form 8938 FATCA | $100-150 |
| Amended 1040-X | $200-350 |
| Back taxes (per year) | $250-500 |

### Diaspora factor

Diaspora market typically pays **30-50% less** than NATP averages:

- Referral/trust clients, not premium marketing
- Competition with informal preparers (charging $80-150)
- Predominantly simple W-2 returns
- Community relationship

### Your pricing strategy

**Year 1 — Starting out (PTIN only)**:

- Simple W-2 1040: $120-150
- 1040 + state: $150-180
- 1040 + Schedule C: $250-300
- Roughly at local informal market level

**Year 2 — With AFSP**:

- Raise 10-15%
- Sell the IRS directory differentiator

**Year 3+ — With EA**:

- Raise 30-50%
- Can match or exceed NATP averages
- Justification: credential + professional service + representation

### How to present the quote to the client

**Option A — Single number** (most common in diaspora):

> "Don Carlos, for your case (W-2 + 2 dependents + VA state) it's $180 total"

**Option B — Breakdown** (for complex cases or educated clients):

```
Base 1040 (W-2)                $120
Schedule A (itemized)           $40
State VA (Form 760)             $50
Child Tax Credit (due diligence) $20
─────────────────────────────────
Total                          $230
```

### Acceptable discounts

- **Referral**: $20 off
- **Returning client** (2+ years): 5-10%
- **Multiple family members**: 10-15% on the 2nd/3rd
- **Early payment** (before March 15): $10 off

### Fee disputes

- Engagement letter protects you
- For non-paying clients: **hold the return** until paid (legal, ethical, documented in engagement letter)
- Don't transmit without payment except in exceptional circumstances with trusted clients
