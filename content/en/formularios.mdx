# Key Forms

This is your basic kit. Know each one well — what it collects, where it goes in the 1040, what common errors exist.

## Income documents the client receives

### <FormRef id="W-2" /> — Wages and Tax Statement

- Employer to employee (by Jan 31)
- Box 1 = wages, Box 2 = federal withholding
- Boxes 3-6 = Social Security and Medicare wages/tax
- Box 12 = special codes (401k = D, HSA = W, etc.)
- Boxes 15-17 = state info

### <FormRef id="1099-NEC" /> — Nonemployee Compensation

- Independent contractors, freelancers
- $600/year minimum threshold to issue
- **Reported on Schedule C**, not as wages
- Box 1 = total amount

### <FormRef id="1099-MISC" /> — Miscellaneous Information

- Rents, royalties, prizes, awards, gross proceeds to attorneys
- Box 1 = rents (→ Schedule E)
- Box 3 = other income (→ Schedule 1)

### <FormRef id="1099-K" /> — Payment Card & Third Party

- From Stripe, PayPal, Venmo Business, Etsy, eBay, Uber, Lyft
- Current federal threshold $5,000 (2024, phasing down to $600)
- **Warning**: may duplicate with 1099-NEC — must reconcile
- → Schedule C typically

### Other 1099s

- **1099-INT** — interest income (Schedule B if > $1,500)
- **1099-DIV** — dividends (Schedule B if > $1,500)
- **1099-R** — retirement distributions (IRA, 401k, pension)
- **SSA-1099** — Social Security benefits (up to 85% taxable)
- **1099-G** — unemployment, state refund
- **1099-B** — broker sales (Schedule D)
- **1099-S** — real estate sale

### Deduction documents

- **1098** — mortgage interest (itemized)
- **1098-T** — tuition (AOTC/LLC education credits)
- **1098-E** — student loan interest (up to $2,500 above-the-line)
- **5498** — IRA contributions (informational)

## 1040 Schedules

| Schedule | Title | What it reports |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Additional Income and Adjustments | Business income, alimony, capital loss, educator expenses, HSA, IRA, student loan interest |
| **2** | Additional Taxes | SE tax, additional Medicare, excess HSA |
| **3** | Additional Credits and Payments | Foreign tax credit, dependent care, education, retirement savings contribution |
| **A** | Itemized Deductions | Medical, SALT (capped $10k), mortgage, charity, casualty |
| **B** | Interest and Dividends | If > $1,500 or foreign accounts |
| **C** | Profit or Loss from Business | Self-employed income and expenses |
| **D** | Capital Gains and Losses | Stock, crypto, real estate sales |
| **E** | Supplemental Income | Rental, royalty, K-1 partnership/S-corp |
| **SE** | Self-Employment Tax | 15.3% on net SE income |

## Special forms for the diaspora

### <FormRef id="W-7" /> — Application for ITIN

For dependents, spouses or the taxpayer themselves without SSN:

- Filed **together with** the 1040 (not separately)
- Documents: passport (original or certified by CAA or issuer) or combination of two (birth cert + foreign ID)
- **CAA (Certifying Acceptance Agent)** can certify documents without sending originals to IRS — business opportunity for the preparer
- Takes 6-11 weeks to receive ITIN

<Callout type="tip" title="CAA: opportunity for your business">
Becoming a CAA lets you certify ITIN documents for clients so they don't have to send their passport to the IRS. It's a powerful differentiator in the diaspora community. Requires Form 13551 and IRS-approved course.
</Callout>

### <FormRef id="8867" /> — Paid Preparer Due Diligence Checklist

**MANDATORY** when claiming:

- EITC
- CTC/ACTC
- AOTC
- HOH filing status

Each omission = **$635 penalty** (2024, indexed annually). **Per return**. Four credits wrong = $2,540 for a single return.

### <FormRef id="8879" /> — IRS e-file Signature Authorization

Client's electronic signature authorizing you to e-file for them. **NOT optional** — client MUST sign before transmission.

- Valid only for the tax year in question
- Must keep for 3 years
- Every schedule/form on the return must be reflected

### <FormRef id="4868" /> — Extension

Covered in ch. 01. Only extends filing, not payment.

### <FormRef id="1040-X" /> — Amended Return

- To correct an already-filed return
- Must include comparison (columns A/B/C: original / change / corrected)
- Must explain changes in Part III
- 3-year limit to claim refund

## Income sources to probe in intake

Mental checklist when interviewing the client:

- [ ] W-2 employment
- [ ] 1099-NEC contractor
- [ ] Self-employment without 1099 (cash)
- [ ] Rental property
- [ ] Investment accounts (brokerage)
- [ ] Crypto activity
- [ ] Retirement distributions
- [ ] Social Security
- [ ] Unemployment
- [ ] Gambling winnings (W-2G)
- [ ] Foreign income (watch FTC / 2555)
- [ ] Foreign accounts (FBAR / 8938)
- [ ] Cancellation of debt (1099-C)
