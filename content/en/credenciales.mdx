# Preparer Credentials from El Salvador

The IRS has several levels of preparer credentials. This is your roadmap as a Salvadoran operating remotely.

## Level 0 — No credential (NOT legal to charge)

In USA you **cannot charge** for preparing tax returns without a PTIN. One exception: VITA/TCE program volunteers (unpaid). Charging without PTIN violates federal law (26 USC §6109).

## Level 1 — PTIN (Preparer Tax Identification Number)

**Mandatory minimum** to charge for preparing or assisting in federal returns.

### What it is

- 9-digit number assigned by IRS (format P + 8 digits)
- Renewed **annually** (cost ~$19.75, adjusted)
- Must appear on **every return** you prepare

### How to get it as a non-resident without SSN

**Problem**: the IRS online system (irs.gov/ptin) asks for SSN. Without SSN you can't use the online process.

**Solution**: paper process via Form W-12 + **Form 8946**

1. **Form W-12** — IRS Paid Preparer Tax Identification Number Application
2. **Form 8946** — PTIN Supplemental Application For Foreign Persons Without a Social Security Number
3. Original identity documents (or certified):
   - Passport + US visa if you have one
   - Or two of: national ID, foreign driver license, birth certificate
4. Mail to: IRS Tax Professional PTIN Processing Center (address on form)
5. Wait 4-6 weeks

<Callout type="info" title="Form 8946 is the key">
Form 8946 is specifically for **foreign persons without SSN**. Without it, the IRS will not process the application.
</Callout>

### Limitations of PTIN alone

- You can PREPARE returns
- You can CHARGE for preparing them
- You **cannot represent** the client before the IRS in audits or collections (except for returns you prepared and signed, with limitations)
- Listed as "PTIN Holder" in the IRS Directory of Federal Tax Return Preparers

## Level 2 — AFSP (Annual Filing Season Program)

**Voluntary**, but recommended. The IRS created it after losing *Loving v. IRS* (2014) which blocked mandatory testing.

### Requirements

- Valid PTIN
- **18 hours of continuing education** annually (from approved provider):
  - 6h Annual Federal Tax Refresher (AFTR) course + test
  - 10h federal tax law
  - 2h ethics
- Consent to Circular 230 practitioner rules

### Benefits

- Listed as "AFSP Record of Completion" in the directory (higher than just PTIN)
- **Limited representation rights** for returns you prepared
- Marketing differentiator

### Approximate cost

- CE courses $100-300
- No additional fee to IRS

## Level 3 — EFIN (Electronic Filing Identification Number)

Separate from PTIN. **Mandatory if you'll file 11+ returns per year** (federal e-file mandate).

### Requirements

- Application via IRS e-Services online
- **Background check + fingerprinting** — HERE is the problem for non-residents
- Suitability check (criminal history, tax compliance, credit)
- 45 days for approval
- **No fee**

<Callout type="danger" title="Fingerprinting from El Salvador">
IRS requires fingerprints taken at an authorized site in USA or at a US embassy. This is a real obstacle. Alternatives:
1. **Travel to USA** for fingerprinting (travel cost)
2. **Partner with a US-based ERO** — another preparer with EFIN transmits for you
3. **Paper filing** (under 11 returns/year, legal)
4. **Use ERO-for-hire filing services**
</Callout>

### ERO alternative

If you can't get EFIN, you can work as a **non-ERO preparer**:

- You prepare the return
- A colleague with EFIN (Electronic Return Originator) transmits it
- Each return signed by both (or just the ERO depending on setup)
- Split fee or ERO charges a transmission fee

## Level 4 — Enrolled Agent (EA) ⭐ RECOMMENDED

**Best option for non-residents** who want a full credential.

### Why EA

- **Federal credential** — recognized in all 50 states
- **Unlimited representation rights** — you can represent clients in audits, appeals, collections, regardless of who prepared the return
- **No university degree required** (unlike CPA)
- **Exam available internationally** at Prometric centers (in San Salvador, Guatemala, Panama)
- Professional prestige

### SEE Exam (Special Enrollment Examination)

Three parts, each independent:

| Part | Topic | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Individuals | ~85 | 3.5h |
| **2** | Businesses | ~85 | 3.5h |
| **3** | Representation, Practices, Procedures | ~85 | 3.5h |

- Cost: ~$267 per part (2024)
- Scaled score 40-130, passing = 105
- Can take in any order
- 2-year window to complete all 3
- Typical prep: 3-6 months study per part

### After the exam

1. Pass all 3 parts
2. **Background check** by IRS
3. Pay enrollment fee (~$140)
4. Receive "Enrolled Agent" designation
5. **72 hours CE every 3 years** (minimum 16/year, 2h ethics/year)
6. **Renewal every 3 years** (~$140)

### Total estimated cost

- Study materials: $400-800 (Gleim, Surgent, Fast Forward Academy)
- 3 exams: ~$800
- Enrollment: $140
- **Total: $1,300-1,800** and ~6-12 months of study

<Callout type="tip" title="Why EA is worth it for you">
As a Salvadoran in ES without SSN, EA gives you:
1. IRS-recognized credential without needing SSN (you CAN be EA without SSN, using a "Preparer Identification Number")
2. IRS representation → you can help clients in audit
3. Clear differentiation from the informal market
4. Justification to raise your fees 30-50%
</Callout>

## Level 5 — CPA (Certified Public Accountant)

- Requires accounting degree (150 credits, BS + master's)
- Uniform CPA exam (4 parts)
- State license (each state has its board)
- 40h CE/year
- **Impractical from El Salvador** unless you already have the degree

## Circular 230

IRS document (Treasury Department Circular 230) regulating preparers. **Mandatory reading** if you'll be AFSP or EA. Covers:

- Duties and restrictions
- Best practices
- Fee structures (includes §10.27 — contingent fee prohibition)
- Return preparer conduct
- Disciplinary procedures

## Suggested roadmap for you

```
Month 0-1:  Get PTIN (Form W-12 + 8946)
Month 1-3:  Prepare first clients (1-5 for experience)
Month 3-4:  Complete AFSP (18h CE) — better directory listing
Month 4-12: Study SEE Part 1 → take exam
Year 2:     Complete Parts 2 and 3 → Enrolled Agent
Year 3+:    Raise fees, expand to other states
```
